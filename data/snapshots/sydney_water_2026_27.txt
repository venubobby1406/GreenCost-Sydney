bill Our prices Residential pricing Residential pricing Pricing for your home Charges apply to houses, flats, strata units, dual occupancies and mixed developments. Mixed developments are properties made up of at least one residential and at least one non-residential property. These charges apply from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027. Typical charges What we charge for Water service charge Wastewater service charge Stormwater service charge Water usage charges Backflow charges Sydney Olympic Park charges Typical charges Typical quarterly charges for a house (water and wastewater services using 50 kL) is $387.03 for a 92 day quarter. Most homes are billed for: water service water usage wastewater service stormwater service (in some areas). Want to see the quarterly charge for your property? Use our fixed charges calculator . Enter your address to get the current charges. Service 2026–27 Charge Water service $26.65 per quarter Water usage $3.41 per kilolitre Wastewater service $189.88 per quarter Stormwater service $30.26 per quarter per house $9.67 per quarter per unit What we charge for For a home, your bill usually includes: fixed charges for being connected to our water, wastewater and stormwater usage charges for the water you use. Fixed charges are billed quarterly but calculated daily. Here's how many days are in each quarter: July and October – 92 days January – 90 days April – 91 days. The charges shown below are based on the longest quarter – 92 days. Need help with your bill? If you're a pensioner who owns and lives in your own home, you may be eligible for a pension rebate on fixed service charges. If you're having payment difficulties, we may be able to offer help with your bill . For full details on who pays what, and when, see our Service and usage charges policy . Water service charge This is the fixed charge you pay for your connection to our water supply, regardless of how much water you use. Charge type Maximum quarterly charge 2025–26 Maximum quarterly charge 2026–27 If you have a meter $29.73 a quarter $26.65 a quarter If you don't have a meter $173.55 a quarter $181.56* a quarter *If dam levels for Greater Sydney drop below 60%, the unmetered charge will increase to $200.93 until dam levels are back over 70%. The water service charge is adjusted each year based on how much water we needed from the rivers and dams managed by WaterNSW . Wastewater service charge You pay a fixed charge for your connection to our wastewater system. This charge is made up of 2 parts: the cost to operate and maintain our wastewater network a deemed amount for usage, which covers the transport and treatment of your wastewater. Charge type Maximum quarterly charge 2025–26 Maximum quarterly charge 2026–27 Your wastewater service $174.40 a quarter $189.88 a quarter Stormwater service charge If your property is in one of our stormwater drainage areas, you'll pay a fixed charge to help maintain our large trunk drains. You might also be charged separately by your local council for street-level drainage. Not sure if this applies to you? Use our stormwater catchment map to check if your property is in a stormwater area. Charge type Maximum quarterly charge 2025–26 Maximum quarterly charge 2026–27 If you live in a unit or are low impact $9.29 $9.67 If you live in a house $29.07 $30.26 Reusing stormwater at home? You might get a discount. If you capture and reuse most of the stormwater on your property, you may be eligible for a discount on your stormwater charges . Explore more ways to use stormwater at your home . Water usage charges If your property has a water meter, you'll be charged each quarter based on how much water you use. Backflow administration fee If your property has a testable backflow prevention device, you'll pay a quarterly fee (one per property) to help cover the cost of our backflow prevention program, which protects the water network. The annual test fee will apply for each device tested. This charge will be raised on your next bill. This charge will be raised on your next bill.
